Ramachandra Ortho Care · Clinic Management System

Reception Cheat Sheet
1. Add Walk-In Patient
  1. Click + Walk-In Patient button in Admin.
  2. Enter Phone Number (auto-detects returning patients).
  3. Enter Name, Age, Gender, and Locality.
  4. Select Consultation (₹350) or Free Review (₹0).
  5. Click Book Appointment. Token is assigned immediately!
2. Print OP Slip (Letterhead)
  1. In today's queue, find the patient row.
  2. Click the 🖨️ Printer Icon next to their name.
  3. Or click the print link in the confirmation email.
  4. Insert pre-printed letterhead into the printer tray.
  5. Press Print (48mm top margin is pre-aligned).
3. Manage Queue Status
  1. When patient arrives at clinic: click Mark Waiting.
  2. When doctor calls patient: status moves to Consulting.
  3. When doctor finishes: click Mark Done.
  4. Collect ₹350 cash / UPI and mark Paid.
4. Reschedule / Cancel
  1. If patient calls to reschedule: click Reschedule and pick a new slot.
  2. If cancelling: click Cancel.
  3. Cancelled appointments move to the "Show Cancelled" dropdown filter.
  4. Automated WhatsApp notification sends immediately.
⭐ 10-Day Free Follow-Up Rule (Important)
  • Every paid consultation includes 1 free review visit within 10 days.
  • Always check if the patient has their previous prescription slip.
  • The system automatically tracks the 10-day validity window and nudges the patient via WhatsApp.

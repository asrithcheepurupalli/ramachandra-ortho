Two ways to pay

Pick whichever is easier on the clinic's cash flow.

Both options land at the same ₹75,000 across the first year. The only real difference is when the money moves, and whether ongoing hosting, WhatsApp costs, and small fixes are bundled in or handled as they come up.

Option A
Pay it in full
One payment settles the build. Nothing recurring afterward.
₹75,000one time
 
  • Settles everything on page 1, in full
  • Small fixes and content updates stay free, same as always
  • Site keeps running on its current hosting at no extra cost
  • A new feature down the line gets scoped and quoted on its own
  • No monthly commitment either way
Option B
Pay over time
Less upfront, with ongoing hosting and support built in.
₹45,000now
plus ₹2,500 / month, ongoing
  • Hosting, domain, and account upkeep, handled
  • WhatsApp message costs from Meta, covered in the monthly fee
  • Unlimited small fixes and content or schedule changes
  • One small new feature included every quarter
  • Same-day response if anything ever breaks
⚖️Over twelve months, Option B totals ₹45,000 + (₹2,500 × 12) = ₹75,000, the same as Option A. Same value, different pacing. Pick whichever suits the clinic's cash flow this quarter.

Hosting, domain & WhatsApp usage
Runs on free-tier infrastructure today, so there's no real bill yet. If that ever changes, Option B's monthly fee already covers it.

The site currently lives at ramachandraorthocare.com. Moving it to a dedicated address, for example ramachandraorthocare.in, is a one-time registration whenever you'd like it.

Notes
No due date and no interest either way. On Option A, this invoice is paid whenever suits you. On Option B, the first ₹45,000 covers the build to date and the ₹2,500 monthly starts whenever you're ready. Switching from A to B later, or the other way round, is fine, just say so.
A note on why this isn't priced per booking
Some clinic software out there charges per appointment, close to ₹10 a booking with no cap. At this clinic's pace of around 1,200 bookings a month, that's roughly ₹12,000 a month, over ₹1,40,000 a year, and it keeps rising as the clinic grows. Others charge a flat subscription instead, anywhere from about ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 a month, regardless of how many patients come through. This was priced as neither. It was a job, charged once for the work it took, not a running cut of the clinic's success or a subscription that never ends. That's the whole reasoning behind the flat ₹75,000 above, whichever option you pick.

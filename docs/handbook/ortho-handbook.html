Module B

RC, the on-site assistant

Short for the clinic itself — RC answers the questions patients usually call the front desk for, right on the website, in English, Telugu, or Hindi, any time of day.

Always-visible, one tap to open

A floating chat button sits on every page. No app to download, no account to create.

  • Doctor-in-today status & today's fee
  • About the doctor — qualifications, experience, rating
  • Consulting hours & location, with one-tap directions
  • What the clinic treats
  • Quick-reply chips — no typing required for common questions

RC runs on the same logic built for the clinic's WhatsApp booking flow — so the answers a patient gets on the site match what they'd get messaging the clinic directly. Nothing to keep in sync by hand.

C. ONLINE BOOKING
08 / 10
Module C

Online appointment booking

A patient can book without calling the clinic at all — pick a day, see real open slots, confirm with their name and phone number.

The /book page — day picker, live 15-minute slots, and the confirmation form.

What makes it reliable

  • Slots are checked against the real database — two patients can never grab the same slot
  • 15-minute appointment windows, matching how the clinic actually runs
  • Closed days and past times are simply not offered — no dead-end bookings
  • A confirmed booking shows up immediately in the admin dashboard's live queue
Why it mattersEvery booking made here is instantly visible to the front desk — no separate system to check, no risk of it getting missed.

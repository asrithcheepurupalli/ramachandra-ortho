How to Use the System

Ramachandra Ortho Care · Front Desk Quick Reference

1
Add a walk-in patient
  • Open ramachandraorthocare.com/admin and log in
  • You will land on the Today tab
  • Scroll down to Walk-in / reserve
  • Enter patient name, phone (optional), age, gender
  • Click Add to queue
  • A token number will be issued automatically
2
Manage today's queue
  • The Today tab shows all patients for the day
  • Use Call next to move a patient to "Waiting"
  • Click the green checkmark to mark a patient as "Consulting"
  • When done, mark them as "Done"
  • Use Broadcast to send a WhatsApp message to all waiting patients
3
Reschedule or cancel
  • Find the patient in today's queue or the Calendar tab
  • Click the circular arrow icon to reschedule
  • Pick a new date and time, then confirm
  • The patient gets an updated WhatsApp message automatically
  • To cancel instead, click the X icon
